Google Analytics uses cookies and collects: a truncated/anonymized IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, time on page, and the referring website. We do not collect names, emails, or other directly identifying information. ## Why we collect it (legal basis) We use this data solely to measure traffic and improve the site. Where required by law (e.g., for EU/UK visitors), our legal basis is your consent, given through the cookie banner shown on your first visit. You can decline, and no analytics cookies will be set. You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking the Cookie Preferences link in the footer. ## How long we keep it Google Analytics retains event data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted. Aggregated reporting data may be retained longer. ## Who else sees it Data is processed by Google LLC on our behalf. Google may transfer data to servers in the United States and other countries. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all sites here: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. ## Your rights Because we only collect anonymous analytics data, we can't identify individual visitors and can't delete or look up specific records. You can stop collection by withdrawing consent in the cookie banner, clearing cookies, or using Google's opt-out tool. EU/UK visitors may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority. ## Changes If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for material changes, post a notice on the site. ## Contact For privacy-related questions, please use contact details in [Impressum](https://christophmolnar.com/impressum).
